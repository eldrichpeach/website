# 🍑 EldrichPeach HQ
# "The Peach Pit"

> **The website is the portfolio.** Instead of reading about what EldrichPeach can build,
> visitors *explore* our studio as a living, interactive 3D office where every room shows off something the team made.

## What is this?

EldrichPeach HQ is the official team website for **EldrichPeach**, a seven-person
dev team on a delightfully ambitious quest to build
**100 games + 100 apps**.

The site's core concept: **an explorable team HQ**. Visitors walk through our
virtual office, poke around, unlock rooms, and discover our work by playing with it.

## 🗺️ The World

| Room | What lives there |
|------|------------------|
| **Lobby** | The mascot greets you at the door (tentacle wiggle on hover, obviously). Click a tentacle to enter. |
| **Office Rooms** | Each team member gets their own room! A personal space showing their role, skills, projects, and flavor. Many secrets to find! |
| **Lounge** | Check the Project Board, Tablet or the Game shelf. |
| **Dungeon** | The portfolio as game levels. Projects become rooms with playable mini-demos. |
| **Behind the Build** | A timeline / activity trail showing how ideas move from concept → collaboration → finished work. |

**🍑 Peach Meter:** a progress bar that fills as you explore. Hit 100% and the mascot throws confetti.
